To compare aggregation at equal capacity, we retrain three baselines on DINOv3-7B, each under its own published recipe with nothing changed but the backbone. Table~\ref{tab:perdataset} gives Recall@1 on all thirteen benchmarks. Our mean is $82.1$ against $73.1$ for the strongest retrained baseline, we are within one point of the best on nine of the thirteen and ahead by more than $14$ on three, and moving from the DINOv2 backbone to the DINOv3 one raises our mean by $1.4$ with nothing re-fitted but the two unsupervised bases.
