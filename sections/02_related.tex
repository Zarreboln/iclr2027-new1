\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Supervised and training-free VPR.}
Supervised VPR learns image representations from geographic labels~\citep{netvlad}. CosPlace and EigenPlaces~\citep{cosplace,eigenplaces} formulate training as classification over geographic cells. MixVPR~\citep{mixvpr} aggregates features through feed-forward mixing, and CliqueMining~\citep{cliquemining} samples positive and negative examples from cliques of visually similar images. 
SALAD~\citep{salad} and BoQ~\citep{boq} develop both aggregation and adaptation strategies based on strong pretrained backbones such as DINOv2~\citep{dinov2}. SuperVLAD~\citep{supervlad} removes cluster-centre subtraction while retaining learned assignments, and EMVP~\citep{emvp} uses second-order features for centroid-free probing. SelaVPR~\citep{selavpr} keeps the backbone frozen and tunes small adapters inserted into it, and SelaVPR++~\citep{selavprpp} trains those adapters without backpropagating through the backbone. CricaVPR~\citep{cricavpr} attends across the images in a batch, so the descriptor of one image depends on the others.

Training-free VPR aggregates local features without any place supervision. DenseVLAD~\citep{densevlad} densely samples local descriptors, and AnyLoc~\citep{anyloc} aggregates frozen DINOv2 patch features with VLAD over a vocabulary fitted per domain, which it found more accurate than GeM pooling~\citep{gem}. SegVLAD~\citep{segvlad} retrieves over segments proposed by SAM~\citep{kirillov2023segment} to accommodate partial scene overlap and supports both generic and task-specialised encoders. The zero-shot variant of EffoVPR~\citep{effovpr} selects frozen patch features by class-token attention and matches them without any place supervision. 
Our approach uses the repetition of patches within each image to determine their contribution and applies the same weights to aggregation and candidate matching.

\subsection{Feature aggregation, weighting and matching} 
Most VPR methods represent an image by a global descriptor aggregated from its local features and retrieve by nearest-neighbour search. 
Fisher vectors~\citep{fisherkernel,improvedfisher} encode each descriptor relative to a learned codebook and sum the encodings per codeword, while second-order pooling~\citep{bcnn,mpncov} averages the outer products of the descriptors into a bilinear or covariance matrix. 
\citet{emk} show that such a pooled descriptor scores an image pair by summing a kernel over all pairs of their local descriptors.  

Discriminative patch mining~\citep{whatmakesparis,modeseeking} asks which local patterns set one image collection apart from another.
In VPR, local features are discarded when they recur across unrelated places~\citep{knopp}, down-weighted when they belong to repeated structures~\citep{repetitive} or scored by their distinctiveness~\citep{dislocation}, and \citet{perlocation} train a classifier per location that learns which features identify it. 
SAGE~\citep{sage} learns a residual weight for each patch from place labels before pooling.
Democratic aggregation~\citep{democratic} weights descriptors without any reference to places, rescaling them iteratively until each contributes equally to the self-similarity of the aggregate. And generalized max pooling~\citep{gmp} solves a ridge regression so that every descriptor has the same similarity to the aggregate.
Other weighting signals include visual-word inverse document frequency~\citep{videogoogle}, class-token attention~\citep{effovpr}, and pair-dependent cross-reference weights~\citep{deepemd}. 
our method balances contributions within the image, giving each block of near-duplicate patches a total weight of one before second-order pooling.
The blocks are read from the image's own self-similarity, so no place label enters the weight.

Two-stage VPR reranks the top-$k$ candidates by comparing the local features of the query with those of each candidate directly. 
Patch-NetVLAD~\citep{patchnetvlad} matches multi-scale patch descriptors with spatial verification, and SelaVPR~\citep{selavpr} counts mutual nearest neighbours between adapted local features. 
R2Former~\citep{r2former} learns reranking from local-feature correlations and spatial information, and Pair-VPR~\citep{pairvpr} trains a transformer to classify whether an image pair depicts the same place. 
FoL~\citep{fol} restricts matching to learned discriminative regions. 
EffoVPR~\citep{effovpr} selects local features by class-token attention and ranks candidates by the number of mutual nearest-neighbour matches above a similarity threshold.
Our method instead scores a candidate by confidence-gated MaxSim, which sums similarities rather than counting matches and trains nothing.
